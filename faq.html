FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things people usually ask before booking a project. Can't find yours? Just ask.

Pricing & payment

What's included in the price?

Every Design + Build package includes a custom design, the coded website, and a set number of revision rounds. Check the Pricing page for exactly what's in Starter, Business and Online Store.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes — a 50% deposit secures your booking and project start date. The remaining balance is due once the site is ready to launch.

What payment methods do you accept?

EFT, or card payment via PayFast or Yoco — whichever's easiest for you. Details are confirmed when you book your call.

Do you offer payment plans beyond the 50% deposit?

For bigger projects, yes — this can be arranged case by case, so just ask on your discovery call.

Process & timeline

How does the process work?

A short discovery call to understand your goals, then a design phase you review and refine, then the build — hand-coded and version-controlled on GitHub, deployed on Vercel.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the package and how quickly content and feedback come through, but most Starter and Business sites are completed within a few weeks of kickoff.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your logo (if you have one), any brand colours or existing materials, the text and images for your pages, and a sense of sites you like the look of.

What if I already have a website?

That's fine — a redesign or rebuild works the same way as a new site, and I can advise on what's worth keeping.

Hosting & after launch

Do I need my own domain and hosting?

You can bring your own, or use one of the Hosting & Care plans, from R250/month — hosting, a .co.za domain, SSL, email and backups included.

Can I edit the site myself after it's built?

Yes — you'll get handoff notes so you can make basic edits yourself, or choose a Care plan that includes monthly edits done for you.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

Business Care and Premium Care plans include updates and security checks as standard. Outside of a plan, just get in touch and I'll take a look.

Do you work with businesses outside South Africa?

Yes — projects are done remotely, and the Pricing page lets you view costs in a few different currencies to make budgeting easier.
