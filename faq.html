Pricing & payment

What's included in the price?

Every Design + Build package includes a custom design, the coded website, the essential pages (privacy and terms, a PAIA manual page where needed, and a custom page-not-found page) and a set number of revision rounds. The essential pages do not count towards your page limit. See the Pricing page for each package.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. A 50% deposit secures your booking and project start date. The remaining balance is due once the site is ready to launch.

What payment methods do you accept?

EFT, or card payment via Paystack, PayFast or Yoco, whichever is easiest for you. Details are confirmed when you book your call.

How much are extra pages?

Extra pages are R850 each. If you need more than 15 pages, or something special like bookings, I'll give you a fixed quote.

For doctors & practices

I'm very busy. How much of my time do you need?

Very little. I send you one form to fill in and ask for the photos you would like used. Everything else goes through me by email or WhatsApp, and I work after hours so your consulting time stays free.

Do you follow the HPCSA's advertising rules?

I build with the HPCSA's advertising rules in mind: factual, not misleading, and no claims such as "best" or "most experienced". Your practice remains responsible for approving the content, and if you are unsure about anything, I recommend checking with your practice manager or professional body.

What about patient privacy and POPIA?

The site does not collect medical history. Booking forms ask only for basic contact details, and a privacy policy page is included. If you want a form on the site, I will keep it to what reception actually needs.

Can I see a preview before I decide?

Yes. I'm happy to send a preview of what your website could look like, with no obligation.

Will my practice show up on Google?

I set the site up properly for Google search and can set up your Google Business Profile, which helps patients nearby find you. I cannot promise a particular ranking, as nobody honestly can.

Process & timeline

How does the process work?

A short call to understand your practice, a form for the details and photos, a design you review and refine, then the build. Nothing goes live without your approval.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the package and how quickly content and feedback come through, but most sites are completed within a few weeks of kickoff.

What do you need from me to get started?

The completed form, your logo if you have one, any brand colours or material you already use, and the photos you would like on the site.

What if I already have a website?

That's fine. A redesign works the same way as a new site, and I can advise on what's worth keeping.

Hosting & after launch

Do I need my own domain and hosting?

You can bring your own, or use one of the Hosting & Care plans, from R250/month, which include hosting, a .co.za domain, SSL, email and backups.

Can I make changes myself?

You can, with handoff notes for basic edits. Most practices prefer to send me changes by WhatsApp or email, and I handle them for you.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

Business Care and Premium Care plans include updates and security checks as standard. Outside of a plan, just get in touch and I'll take a look.

Do you work with practices outside Hoedspruit?

Yes. I'm based in Hoedspruit and work with practices across South Africa. Everything is done remotely by email, WhatsApp and call.
