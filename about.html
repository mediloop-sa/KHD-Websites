About

Hi, I'm Kea.

I'm Kea Dilebo — a web designer and developer, and the person behind KHD Websites. I'm just a girl who deeply loves coding and creativity, and KHD Websites is where those two worlds get to live together: clean, working code wrapped around a design that actually feels like something.

I write every line myself, version it on GitHub, and ship it on Vercel — so what you see in review is exactly what goes live.
