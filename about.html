About

Hi, I'm Kea.

I'm Kea Dilebo, a web designer and developer, and the person behind KHD Websites. By day I'm a full-time somatologist, so I work in the health and wellness world every day. Alongside that, I design and build software, including my own booking and management platform for the spa industry.

That mix is why I focus on specialist doctors and private practices. I know how busy your days are, how much patients rely on trust, and how much a clear, calm website can help. I write every line myself, version it on GitHub and publish it on Vercel, so what you approve in review is exactly what goes live.
