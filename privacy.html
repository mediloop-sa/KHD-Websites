Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Who we are

KHD Websites is operated by Keabetswe "Kea" Dilebo, based in South Africa. We're committed to handling your personal information responsibly, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). For anything related to this policy, contact khdwebsites@gmail.com.

2. What information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, such as when you fill in the contact form on this site or email/message us. This typically includes:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your approximate budget (if provided)
  • Details about your project, shared in your message

If you go on to become a client, we may also collect additional information needed to deliver the project and invoice you — such as your business details, billing information, and the content, images or logins you provide for your website.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and discuss your project
  • Scope, build and deliver website design, build and hosting services
  • Invoice you and process payments
  • Communicate with you about your project or account

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for purposes unrelated to the above without asking you first.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. If that changes in future — for example, if we add website analytics to understand visitor traffic — this policy will be updated to reflect it, and, where required, we'll ask for your consent.

5. Sharing your information

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We may share it with trusted third parties strictly where needed to deliver our services, including:

  • Payment processors (PayFast, Yoco) — to process payments you make to us
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers (such as Vercel, or your chosen domain registrar) — where needed to build, host or deploy your website
  • Email providers — to communicate with you

These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function, and are subject to their own privacy terms.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, and client project information for as long as needed to deliver the project, maintain any ongoing hosting or care plan, and meet reasonable business and legal record-keeping needs. You can ask us to delete information we hold about you at any time, subject to section 8 below.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold, including limiting access to it and using reputable third-party providers for hosting, payments and communication. No method of storing or transmitting data online is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we're not legally required to keep it
  • Object to how we're using your information
  • Lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator if you believe your information has been mishandled

To exercise any of these rights, email khdwebsites@gmail.com.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and individuals who are legally able to enter into a contract. This site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our services or legal obligations change. The "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

11. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, reach out via khdwebsites@gmail.com or the Contact page.
